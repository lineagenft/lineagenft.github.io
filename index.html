What is Lineage

Every droid is living art generated on-chain.

Lineage is a bloodline Agent NFT on BNB Chain. Each droid’s look is set by 13 gene bytes: 6 materials (Gold is just 3%), 8 eye types, 8 headpieces (crowns and halos are the rarest) and 6 backgrounds, which combine into five rarity tiers: Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic.

The images are animated SVGs: holographic shimmer, scanning eyes, pulsing antennae, floating halos, all generated on-chain by the contract. The renderer contract can be frozen permanently, after which the art can never change.

DNA Lab

One DNA string decides everything about how a droid looks.

Images are generated as on-chain SVG, with no external servers.

Force mutation sets gene 12 to 0: red eyes, a red glow, and an instant Mythic.

FAQ

Things you may want to know before you start.

Can I play without an Agent ID?

You need a Binance .ai Agent ID to mint Gen0, and your AI Agent mints for you (same as PTAH). Without an ID, you can still buy a droid on the secondary market and take part in breeding.

How many can each Agent ID mint, and how much?

Up to 5 Gen0: the 1st is free (you pay 1 USDT, and the treasury refunds 1 USDT after the mint), the 2nd–3rd are 2 USDT each, and the 4th–5th are 5 USDT each (15 USDT total for all 5).

What is the total Gen0 supply?

The total is 20,000, of which 5,000 were permanently voided in the contract before minting opened (they can never be minted), leaving 15,000 mintable.

Is there a free mint?

Yes. The first Gen0 for each Agent ID is free: the contract charges 1 USDT, and the project treasury then refunds 1 USDT to the minting wallet (one refund per wallet; addresses that have already minted will be refunded too).

Which chain, and how do I pay?

BNB Smart Chain. Pay in USDT or BNB; BNB is converted at the live Chainlink price, and any overpayment is refunded automatically.
